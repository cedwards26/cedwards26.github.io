About Me
I'm a recent Yale graduate (B.S. Environmental Studies, Urban Environments concentration) working at the intersection of flood risk, climate resilience, and urban planning. My senior thesis modeled how urbanization patterns shape flood risk in Washington D.C. and its suburbs, and my GIS work spans remote sensing, hydrologic modeling, and spatial analysis of environmental justice issues.

I spent the last two years working at the Urban Resources Initiative, an urban forestry organization in New Haven, coordinating outreach, managing tree tracking databases, and, of course, planting trees.

I'm currently based in New York City, looking for opportunities to help solve urban resilience challenges.
